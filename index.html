ABOUT OUR EDUCATIONAL TRAVEL EXPERIENCES

FUNDECOR, or the Foundation for the Development of the Central Volcanic Mountain Range (Fundación para el Desarrollo de la Cordillera Volcánica Central) is a Non-Governmental Ecology and Sustainability Organization which handles core operations for the ecological protection, education, research, and sustainability efforts in Costa Rica.

FUNDECOR has a diverse team of experts in each of these fields, with specialists ranging from Geology to Forestry to Ornithology, and we want to work with YOU in educating the world in how to protect our heart and soul: Mother Nature.

MEET SOME OF OUR TEAM

FEDERICO

Federico runs the education program at FUNDECOR. He started out as a tour guide for other companies and was recruited to FUNDECOR in order to be a part of the program. He specialized in history but has a vast knowledge of Costa Rica’s natural life as well.

MANUEL

Manuel is a biologist and water resource specialist who has worked with the education program and Agua Tica over his 25 years working here. He is knowledgeable about a wide range of topics including geology. He has been a tour guide for many field trips and loves sharing his expertise with the kids.

PEDRO

Pedro is the manager of Innovation and business at FUNDECOR. He has worked with many of the forestry projects both as a forest engineer and as a manager. He also focuses on climate change. He works with student groups on sustainable farming and forest management practices.
